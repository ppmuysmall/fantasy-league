How this works

  • Luck Quadrant. Every game is scored two ways: did you win your actual matchup, and did your score beat the league median that week? Deserved Win — you won and were above median. Lucky Win — you won, but your score was below median; you beat a team having a worse week than you. Unlucky Loss — you lost despite being above median; you ran into someone's best week. Deserved Loss — you lost and were below median too.
  • Median Record. Your record if, instead of playing your actual opponent, you just needed to beat the league median every week — a rough stand-in for "how a replacement-level schedule would have treated you."
  • Luck Rating. Actual Wins − Median Wins. Positive means your record is better than your scoring deserves; negative means it's worse.
  • All-Play record. How many of the other 9 teams you'd have beaten that week if everyone played everyone — the least schedule-dependent measure of how good a team actually is.
